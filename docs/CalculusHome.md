#### Everything You Should Know About Calculus Before Your First Day of Calculus Class

Both of my kids will be starting calculus class next year. Calculus is a beautiful subject, but sometimes the beauty of calculus doesn't shine through in a highschool calculus class.  The highlevel ideas and concepts get lost when working intricate and complex problems written in baffling and unfamiliar notation.  In calculus we learn two tasks: taking derivatives and computing integrals.  However, a majority of the class is memorizing (and hopefully deriving) short cut rules for computing these things.  When the class is over, students may be more likely to remember the short cut rules than the higher level ideas from which they are derived.  The focus of this primer is on the high level ideas.   If you have the high level ideas firmly in your head before you start, you can focus on the details of intricate problem solving without losing the big picture.
